`control-points.json`, `commandery-review.json`, `source-coast.json`,
`collated-atlas.js`, and `collation-audit.json` are adaptations or accompanying registration data for
this map, released under **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Changes by this project (2026-11-08):
extract boundary strokes; remove background, lettering, symbols and legend;
sample curves; fit an affine transformation to the project's approximate place
index; transcribe Chinese commandery labels, aliases and source affiliations; join small
drafting gaps, merge drawing slivers, polygonize closed areas and preserve open
frontiers and emigré labels without invented individual polygons; derive state
areas and shared boundaries. The source includes relief credited to **Koba-chan**;
`source-coast.json` traces the modern coast in that background (sea/land color
segmentation, 7-pixel closing, 1.5-pixel simplification), not an ancient coast. Attribution and license links must
accompany redistributed adaptations. No endorsement by the original author is
implied. This notice concerns these reference-map assets, not third-party base
maps; see [../SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) for their separate terms.

These are reference-map adaptations, **not a verified historical GIS dataset**.
The nominal year is 219; some provincial lines in the source use post-220
arrangements. The collated output has 117 closed reference areas, 14 open-boundary records
and 2 emigré labels. Name and affiliation review is distinct from historical
certification of every border.
